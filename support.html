Yes. VOYD Fitness is completely free to download on both the App Store and Google Play. Core features are always free. Optional premium features may be offered in the future, and we'll be transparent when that happens.
VOYD Fitness supports iOS 16 and above, and Android 10 and above. We recommend keeping your device and the app up to date for the best experience. Older OS versions may work but are not officially supported.
Your data is encrypted end-to-end and never sold or shared with third parties. VOYD Fitness collects only what is necessary to provide the service. You can request to export or delete your data by sending us an email.
Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen in the app and enter your email address. You'll receive a reset link within a couple of minutes.
Sign in with the same VOYD Fitness account on both your devices. Changes sync automatically and near-instantly - typically under 200ms. Sync works over Wi-Fi and mobile data. Offline changes are queued and pushed when you reconnect.
Yes. VOYD Fitness is fully functional offline. All your data is cached locally and the full feature set is available without an internet connection. Changes sync automatically as soon as you're back online.
